AI Extension Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Information the extension handles

AI Extension stores provider API keys in local browser extension storage. Keys are sent directly to the provider you select when you make a request. They are not sent to an AI Extension developer server.

Prompts and conversation messages are sent to the selected provider to generate responses. AI Extension does not save chat history.

If you click “Analyze this page,” the extension reads the active webpage’s title, URL, and up to 14,000 characters of page text, then includes that information in a prompt sent to your selected provider. This happens only when you use that feature.

Who receives information

Requests go directly to the provider you selected: OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude. The selected provider receives the API key and request data needed to provide its service. Its handling of that information is governed by its own terms and policies.

This version does not send API keys, prompts, or webpage content to an AI Extension developer-operated server. It does not include accounts, chat-history syncing, analytics, or advertising.

Your choices

You choose whether to enter and save a provider key and whether to analyze a webpage. Use “Forget key” to remove a saved key. Chat messages are kept only in the open extension session and are not saved as chat history by the extension.
